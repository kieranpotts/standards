== Comparison

The choice between approaches is driven by constraints:

[cols="1,3,2"]
|===
| Approach | Fits when | Cost

| Big-bang | Small scope; downtime acceptable | Lowest
| Phased | Natural partition key; partial rollback acceptable | Moderate
| Zero-downtime | Strict availability requirement | Highest
|===

A migration SHOULD default to the simplest approach that meets the requirement. See <<overview>>.
