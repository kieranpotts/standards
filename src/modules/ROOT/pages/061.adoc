= TS-61: AI tools
:toc: macro
:toc-title: Contents

This technical standard covers best practices for using AI tools in software development workflows.

"AI tools" is not one technology. It covers a range of technologies, including harnesses and user interfaces, built
around many different models and providers. The common factor across all of them is a large language model at the core.

The scope of this technical standard runs from choosing a model and an interface, through harness and context
engineering and the design of AI-assisted and agentic workflows, to evaluation, cost optimization, and security.

Out-of-scope is the integration of AI into software applications. This technical standard is scoped to the use of AI
tools to assist in the development, maintenance, and operation of software applications — those applications may or may
not have AI systems integrated into them.

The acronyms LLM and AI are used interchangeably throughout. The subject is AI tools based around large language models
models that are themselves based on the transformer architecture, or similar.
