= Emphasis (bold and italics)

Use bold for UI elements the reader must interact with: button labels, menu items, field names.

Also use bold to introduce a new term at its point of definition, the first time it is used in a document.

Also use bold for a short lead-in term at the start of a list item, terminated by a full stop. Example: "*Performance.* The system responds within 100 ms."

Do not bold phrases in running prose for emphasis. Bold scattered through a paragraph competes with itself, and the reader stops treating any of it as important.

Use italics sparingly, and only for genuine emphasis of a word within a sentence. For foreign words and phrases, see Foreign words.

Do not stack multiple forms of emphasis, eg. bold and italic. Pick one form of emphasis.
