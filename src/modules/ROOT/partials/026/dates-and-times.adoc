= Dates and times

Write dates in ISO 8601-1:2019 format (`2026-07-02`) in technical documentation, since it sorts correctly as a string and is unambiguous across locales.

Avoid the `MM/DD/YYYY` and `DD/MM/YYYY` formats entirely, as both are ambiguous without knowing the writer's locale.

Write a time of day in 24-hour format (`14:30`). It sorts correctly and is unambiguous across locales, unlike `2:30pm` or `2.30pm`. Where a document has to show 12-hour format for a non-technical audience, write it as `2:30pm`, with no space before the lowercase `am`/`pm` and no periods. Separate hours from minutes with a colon, not a period (`6:30pm`, not `6.30pm`), and omit the minutes on the hour (`1am`, not `1:00am`). Do not mix 12-hour and 24-hour formats within one document.
