= Gerunds

A gerund is a verb form ending in "-ing" that functions as a noun in a sentence.

Put a noun or pronoun that modifies a gerund in the possessive case when the action, not the person, is the focus of the sentence. Example: "We appreciated Priya's explaining the tradeoffs." The explaining is the point, not Priya. Not: "We appreciated Priya explaining the tradeoffs."
