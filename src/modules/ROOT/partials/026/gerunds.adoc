= Gerunds

A *gerund* is a verb form ending in "-ing" that functions as a noun in a sentence.

Put a noun or pronoun that modifies a gerund in the possessive case when the action, not the person, is the focus of the sentence.

----
✅ We appreciated Priya's explaining the tradeoffs.
❌ We appreciated Priya explaining the tradeoffs.
----

In the ✅ example, the explaining is the point, not Priya.
