= Significance claims

Show that something matters by stating the fact that makes it matter, not by asserting its importance. Phrases such as "stands as a testament to," "marks a pivotal moment," "underscores the importance of," and "plays a crucial role" claim significance without demonstrating it. State what happened and let the reader judge.

Bad: "The migration marks a pivotal moment in the platform's evolution."

Good: "The migration removes the last dependency on the legacy queue."
