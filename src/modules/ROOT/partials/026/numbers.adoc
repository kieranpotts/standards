= Numbers

Spell out numbers zero through nine in prose: zero, one, two, three... nine. Use numerals from 10 upward: 10, 11, 12...

Spell out an ordinal from first through ninth. Use figures with the suffix from 10th upward. Exaples: "the third retry", "the 12th request".

For a numeric range, see Dashes.

Always use numerals for measurements, versions, and anything paired with a unit, eg. `18 GB`, `v2`, `3 retries`, `20%`.

Hyphenate a spelled-out fraction used as a modifier — eg. "a two-thirds majority" — but not a figure fraction.

Write "percentage points", not "percent", when stating the difference between two percentages. Example: "latency dropped 5 percentage points, from 20% to 15%". Writing "dropped 5 percent" would mean a relative drop to 19%.
