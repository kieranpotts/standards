= Numbers

Spell out numbers zero through nine in prose: zero, one, two, three... nine. Use numerals from 10 upward: 10, 11, 12...

Spell out an ordinal from first through ninth. Use numerals with the suffix from 10th upward. Examples: "the third retry", "the 12th request".

Write integers up to 999,999 in full, with a comma as the thousands separator: "12,500 requests". From one million upward, abbreviate sums of money, quantities, and inanimate objects with "m" or "bn", with no space and at most one decimal place: "£10m", "3.2bn rows". For people and animals, spell out "million" or "billion" with a space: "1 million users". Do not abbreviate where "m" could be misread as a unit, such as metres or minutes.

For a numeric range, see Dashes.

Always use numerals for measurements, versions, and anything paired with a unit, eg. "18 GB", "v2", "3 s", "20%".

Hyphenate a spelled-out fraction used as a modifier (eg. "a two-thirds majority"), but not a fraction written in numerals.

Write "percentage points", not "percent", when stating the difference between two percentages. Example: "latency dropped 5 percentage points, from 20% to 15%". Writing "dropped 5 percent" would mean a relative drop to 19%.
