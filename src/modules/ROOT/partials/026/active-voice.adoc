= Active voice

Prefer *active voice* over passive voice. "The server rejects invalid requests" is clearer than "invalid requests are rejected by the server." Recognize passive voice by its verb phrase, a form of "be" — eg. "am", "is", "are", "was", "were", "been" — followed by a past participle, with the sentence's subject receiving the action rather than performing it. As a check, try appending "by <actor>". A passive sentence will accept it without changing meaning, eg. "invalid requests are rejected [by the server]".
