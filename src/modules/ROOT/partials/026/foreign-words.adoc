= Foreign words

Italicize a foreign word or phrase that has not been absorbed into everyday English usage, and preserve its native accents and diacritics. Example: "the patch delivers the _coup de grâce_ to the legacy parser".

Do not italicize a loanword that has become standard English vocabulary, such as "cafe" or "role". Write these in plain roman type.

Where a word's status is genuinely ambiguous, follow its spelling in an authoritative English dictionary rather than the source language's orthography.
