= Foreign words

Italicize a foreign word or phrase that has not been absorbed into everyday English usage, and preserve its native accents and diacritics: "the API uses a _naïve_ Bayes classifier." Do not italicize a loanword that has become standard English vocabulary, such as "cafe" or "role" — write these in plain roman type. Where a word's status is genuinely ambiguous, follow its spelling in an authoritative English dictionary rather than the source language's orthography.
