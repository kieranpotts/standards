= Units of measure

Leave a space between a numeral and its unit ("10 ms", not "10ms"), except for percentages ("10%") and degrees, which are conventionally set without a space.

Use consistent units within a document. Do not mix, for example, milliseconds and seconds for durations of the same kind of thing without a clear reason.

Give measures in metric (SI) units. Where the audience may expect imperial or US customary units, add the conversion in brackets at first mention only: "50 km (30 miles)". Match the precision of the original. Convert an approximate figure to an equally approximate one ("about 50 km apart" becomes "30 miles", not "31.07 miles"), and likewise for rounded currency amounts.
