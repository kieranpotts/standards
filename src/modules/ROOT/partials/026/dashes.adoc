= Dashes

Use an en dash (`–`), not a hyphen or an em dash, for a numeric range, with no surrounding spaces. Example: "pages 12–20". Use a minus sign (`−`, U+2212), not a hyphen or an en dash, for a negative number. Example: "a range of −5 to 5".

Use an em dash (`—`), surrounded on each side by a single empty space, to set off a parenthetical. Example: "the default retry limit — three attempts — applies to every request". Do not use an em dash to join two independent clauses. See Sentences and paragraphs.

Use em dashes sparingly. A paragraph with several internal asides reads as fragmented and is hard to follow. Most asides read better set off by commas or parentheses, or moved to a sentence of their own. For example, avoid: "The service — after some hesitation — retries the request and logs the outcome." Prefer: "The service, after some hesitation, retries the request and logs the outcome." Or: "The service retries the request, after some hesitation, and logs the outcome."
