= Dashes

Use an em dash (`—`) closed up against the words on either side, with no surrounding spaces, to set off a parenthetical or an abrupt break in a sentence: "the change is backward compatible—no client update is required."

Use em dashes sparingly. A paragraph with several dash-set asides reads as fragmented and makes each aside harder to follow. Most asides read better set off by commas or parentheses, or moved to a sentence of their own.

Use an en dash (`–`), not a hyphen, for a numeric range or a negative number where a hyphen could be misread as a minus sign or a compound modifier: "pages 12–20," "a range of -5 to 5." Do not use an en dash as a substitute for an em dash.
