= Apostrophes

Form a singular possessive by adding `'s`, including for a name ending in `s`. Examples: "the API's rate limit", "Chris's PR". Form a plural possessive by adding only an apostrophe after the existing `s`. Example: "the servers' response times".

Form the plural of an abbreviation or acronym by adding a lowercase "s", with no apostrophe. Examples: "three APIs", "two CDNs", not "three API's".

Write a decade without an apostrophe before the `s`. Example: "the 2020s", not "the 2020's".

Treat a time span before a noun like "experience" or "notice" as a plural possessive, not a compound modifier. Examples: "20 years' experience", "three weeks' notice", not "20 years experience", "three weeks notice". The time span is the thing the noun belongs to — "experience of 20 years".

This differs from a compound adjective built from a number and a unit, which takes a hyphen and no apostrophe. Example: "a 20-year-old system".
