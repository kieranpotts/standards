= Commas

Use the serial comma, also known as the Oxford comma, before the final item in a list of three or more. Example: "create, read, and delete", not "create, read and delete". Omitting the final comma can make the last two items read as a single pair.

Follow an introductory phrase or clause with a comma before the main clause, unless the phrase is short (three words or fewer) and the sentence reads unambiguously without one.

----
After the gateway authenticates the request, it forwards the request to the origin.
First look at the logs.
----

Do not splice two independent clauses together with only a comma. Either join them with a coordinating conjunction (`and`, `but`, `so`), split them into two sentences, or use a semicolon.

----
✅ The request failed, so the client retried it.
❌ The request failed, the client retried it.
----
