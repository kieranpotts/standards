= Filler words

Avoid using words like "very", "actually", "basically", "simply", "just", "quite", "really", "in particular", and "obviously". Rarely do these words add any meaning to the sentence they sit in. Avoid "so" as an intensifier, as in "so fast". The conjunction "so", as in "The request failed, so the client retried it", is fine.

Shorten a wordy phrase to its plain equivalent. Write "to", not "in order to"; "because", not "due to the fact that"; "now", not "at this point in time"; "can", not "has the ability to".

Delete "it is important to note that" outright, and let the sentence that follows carry its own weight.
