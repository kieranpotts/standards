This technical standard covers best practices for using AI tools in software development workflows.

"AI tools" is not one technology. It covers a range of technologies, including but not limited to xref:garden::agent-harness.adoc[*agent harnesses*] built around many different models and model providers. The common factor across all AI tools is a xref:garden::large-language-model.adoc[*large language model*] is integrated into them.

The scope of this technical standard runs from choosing a model, through tuning model behavior, xref:garden::prompt-engineering.adoc[*prompt*] and xref:garden::context-engineering.adoc[*context engineering*], xref:garden::agent-harness.adoc[*harness engineering*], and xref:garden::loop-engineering.adoc[*loop engineering*], to the design of AI-assisted and agentic workflows, including evaluation, cost optimization, and security.

Out-of-scope is the integration of AI into software applications. This technical standard is scoped to the use of AI tools to assist in the development, maintenance, and operation of software applications. Those applications may or may not themselves have AI components integrated into them.

The acronyms *LLM* and *AI* are used interchangeably throughout. The subject is AI tools based around large language models based on the xref:garden::transformer.adoc[*transformer*] or similar architecture.
