[#quote-blocks]
= Quote blocks

If you want to quote sentences or statements, use a quote block. A quote block is delimited by four underscores, and it is labeled with a `quote` attribute list that names who is being quoted and, optionally, the work the quotation is taken from.

[source,asciidoc]
----
[quote, James Baldwin, As Much Truth As One Can Bear]
____
Not everything that is faced can be changed. But nothing can be changed until it is faced.
____
----

For poetry, lyrics, and other text whose line breaks are part of the content, write `verse` in place of `quote`. A verse block preserves the line breaks and indentation of its content.

[source,asciidoc]
----
[verse, William Blake, Songs of Experience]
____
Tiger, tiger, burning bright
In the forests of the night,
What immortal hand or eye
Could frame thy fearful symmetry?
____
----

The Markdown-style blockquote, where each line is prefixed with `>`, SHOULD NOT be used. The "air quotes" syntax, where the quotation sits between two lines of two double quotes (`""`), MUST NOT be used. Asciidoctor 2 does not recognize it, and renders the text as an ordinary paragraph with the quote marks left in.
