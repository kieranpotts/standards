= Responsibility

Requirements are derived from the business domain and SHOULD be written so
business stakeholders can understand them.

The line between business requirement and technical design is often blurry.
"Passwords must be strong" is a business requirement, but it implies rules a
business stakeholder may still care about, even without owning the
implementation:

* Minimum 8 characters.
* Mix of letters (any case) and numbers.
* At least one special character, eg. %^&*().
* No dictionary words.

For this reason, requirements specifications SHOULD be written and maintained
in collaboration between the customer (or a representative, such as a product
owner) and the software development team.

However, ultimately ownership of software requirement specifications lies with
the technical teams. They have responsibility to maintaining the SRS and keeping
it synchronized with production.
