= Statelessness

JWTs MUST be designed to contain all necessary claims for authentication and
authorization, such that no external database lookups – or requests to external
auth services – are required for authentication and validation of users.

JWTs are specifically intended for stateless authentication and authorization.
The stateless nature of JWT-based auth allows for low latency auth processes,
easier horizontal scaling of applications, and reduced complexity in distributed
systems.

If stateful authentication is required, such as when needing to track user
sessions or manage complex state, then JWTs are (probably) not the most
appropriate solution.
